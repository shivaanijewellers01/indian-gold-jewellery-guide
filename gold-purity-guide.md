
# Gold Purity Guide – Understanding 24K, 22K, 18K and 916 Gold

Gold purity is an important factor to understand before purchasing gold jewellery. In India, gold is commonly described using karat (K) and fineness numbers.

## 1. What is 24K Gold?

24-karat gold is approximately 99.9% pure gold. It is relatively soft and is commonly used for coins and bars.

## 2. What is 22K Gold?

22-karat gold contains approximately 91.6% pure gold and is commonly known as 916 gold.

It is widely used in traditional Indian jewellery, including necklaces, bangles, earrings and rings.

## 3. What is 18K Gold?

18-karat gold contains 75% pure gold and 25% other metals. It is commonly used in diamond and gemstone jewellery.

## 4. Gold Purity Comparison

| Gold Type | Approximate Gold Purity |
|---|---:|
| 24K | 99.9% |
| 22K | 91.6% |
| 18K | 75% |
| 14K | 58.5% |

## 5. What Does 916 Mean?

The number 916 represents 916 parts of gold per 1,000 parts of the alloy. This is equivalent to 91.6% gold content.

## 6. Tips Before Buying Gold Jewellery

- Check the applicable BIS hallmark.
- Verify the HUID where applicable.
- Ask for a detailed purchase invoice.
- Check the declared purity and net gold weight.
- Understand making charges and other applicable costs.

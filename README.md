## 1. Understanding Gold Purity

Gold purity is commonly measured in karats (K).

| Gold Type | Purity |
|---|---:|
| 24K | 99.9% (approximately) |
| 22K | 91.6% |
| 18K | 75% |
| 14K | 58.5% |

## 2. What is 916 Gold?

916 gold is commonly known as 22-karat gold. It contains 91.6% gold, with the remaining percentage made up of other metals.

It is widely used in traditional Indian gold jewellery.

## 3. Gold Hallmarking in India

Gold hallmarking helps consumers understand the purity of gold jewellery.

Before purchasing gold jewellery, consumers should:
- Check the purity marking.
- Look for the applicable BIS hallmark.
- Verify the HUID where applicable.
- Ask for a proper purchase invoice.

Refer to the official Bureau of Indian Standards website for current hallmarking information: https://www.bis.gov.in/

## 5. Silver and Platinum Jewellery

Silver jewellery is popular for daily wear, gifting and traditional occasions.

Platinum is valued for its durability and naturally white appearance.

## 6. Jewellery Care Tips

1. Store jewellery in separate soft-lined boxes.
2. Keep jewellery away from harsh chemicals.
3. Remove jewellery before swimming or using cleaning products.
4. Clean using suitable methods recommended by a qualified jeweller.
5. Get jewellery inspected periodically.
